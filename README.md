## 🩺 NisaCare AI
NisaCare – AI‑Powered Women’s Health Companion for Pakistani Women

NisaCare was born from a simple truth: millions of Pakistani women suffer in silence because they lack access to private, non‑judgmental health information.
With NisaCare, Girls opens her phone and types in Urdu: (My periods are very painful). NisaCare’s AI chatbot, followed by trusted medical guidelines, replies immediately in Urdu with practical home care tips and when to see a doctor – all without any judgment.

Girls can also log thier cycle, track her nutrition (iron and vitamin D), and learn about breast cancer self‑examination – all completely anonymously. No login required, no data shared.

NisaCare helps women take charge of their health privately, affordably, and in their own language. It reduces misinformation, breaks taboos, and empowers women to seek timely medical help.
